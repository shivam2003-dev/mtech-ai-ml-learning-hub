## Narrative and teaching quality

The chapter follows a clear progression:

1. problem and motivation;
2. notation and conceptual map;
3. intuitive and formal models;
4. a worked affine-transform case;
5. executable implementations;
6. failure modes and boundary cases;
7. evidence, cost, and research connections;
8. study lab, assignment, oral questions, summary, and next steps.

The one-minute version, Mira running example, chapter promise, quick map,
worked numerical case, common failures, and takeaways prevent the chapter from
reading like a disconnected checklist. Important symbols are introduced before
use, analogies include limits, and implementation details are connected back to
the mathematics.

## Curriculum and claim scope

The curriculum notice explicitly says that the official brochure establishes
only the subject title and Semester 1 placement. The chapter is labelled
`prerequisite`, and supplementary teaching choices are not presented as
university-prescribed units. Source claims, direct derivations, educational
interpretation, and local engineering guidance remain distinguishable.

The chapter does not overclaim research results. Its no-foundational-paper note
for notation conventions is candid and academically sound. Security, privacy,
fairness, and deployment recommendations are framed as guidance rather than
fabricated consensus.
