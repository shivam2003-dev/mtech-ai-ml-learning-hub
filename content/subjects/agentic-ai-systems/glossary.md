# Glossary

- **Agent:** A stateful system that observes, acts through constrained tools,
  receives results, and follows a stopping rule.
- **Action:** A typed proposal to call a tool, ask a question, answer, or stop.
- **Evidence:** A source-backed observation that can be inspected or replayed.
- **Grounding:** Connecting generated claims to retrieved or otherwise approved
  source material.
- **Memory:** Persisted working, episodic, semantic, or procedural information
  with an access and retention policy.
- **Plan-and-Execute:** A controller that creates an explicit plan and dispatches
  its steps through an executor.
- **Prompt injection:** Untrusted content attempting to change instructions or
  permissions.
- **ReAct:** A reasoning-and-acting pattern that interleaves actions and
  observations.
- **Reflexion:** A feedback pattern in which an agent records an outcome and
  revises a subsequent attempt.
- **Stopping rule:** A tested predicate that terminates, refuses, escalates, or
  reports an incomplete task.
- **Tool contract:** A schema, permission boundary, timeout, and output format
  for an external operation.
- **Trace:** Ordered state, action, result, and event records from one run.
