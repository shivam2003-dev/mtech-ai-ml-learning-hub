# Agentic AI Systems

> **Curriculum boundary:** The BITS Pilani WILP brochure verifies *Agentic AI
> Systems* as an elective with a course-topic description. The topics below
> follow that verified description: foundations and use cases; LLMs as agents;
> prompting; ReAct; Plan-and-Execute; Reflexion; memory; knowledge grounding;
> multi-agent collaboration; safety and explainability; evaluation, deployment,
> MLOps; and a goal-driven tool-calling agent. Semester placement remains
> configurable between Semesters 2 and 3. Examples and projects are an
> educational interpretation, not additional official units.

## Reference chapter

1. [A Useful Agent Knows When to Stop](./chapters/01-a-useful-agent-knows-when-to-stop/) — a narrative introduction to state, planning, tool use, memory, evidence, and safety.

## Proposed book sequence

1. A Useful Agent Knows When to Stop
2. ReAct and Plan-and-Execute Control Loops
3. Memory, Context, and Knowledge Grounding
4. Multi-Agent Collaboration Without Lost Accountability
5. Safety, Governance, and Explainability
6. Evaluating, Deploying, and Operating Agents
7. Reproducible Agentic-AI Research

Only Chapter 1 is authored in this vertical slice. Future chapters remain
planned until their source, mathematics, code, editorial, rendering, and
accessibility reviews pass.
