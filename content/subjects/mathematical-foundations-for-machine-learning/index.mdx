# Mathematical Foundations for Machine Learning

> **Curriculum status:** the official programme brochure verifies the subject title and its Semester 1 placement, but it does not provide a topic-level syllabus. The outline and chapter on this page are a **provisional educational interpretation**. Every topic is labelled as a Prerequisite or Supplementary topic and must not be presented as an official university unit.

Machine learning papers and implementations compress a great deal of meaning into a few symbols. A model may be written as a function, a dataset as a matrix, and a batch computation as indexed sums or tensor operations. This book develops the habits needed to unpack that compression: define the objects, state their domains and shapes, follow each transformation, and test whether the final expression is meaningful.

## Available reference chapter

1. [Mathematical Language for Machine Learning](./chapters/01-mathematical-language-for-machine-learning/) — **Prerequisite with Supplementary practice**. Sets, functions, logic, quantifiers, index notation, shapes, counterexamples, and dimension-safe Python.
