# Preface

This provisional book exists to make the mathematical language of machine learning readable, checkable, and usable. The official programme brochure confirms the subject title, **Mathematical Foundations for Machine Learning**, and its Semester 1 placement. It does not publish detailed units for this subject. The book therefore labels planned material as Prerequisite, Supplementary, Advanced extension, or Industry application rather than treating it as official syllabus text.

The first production reference chapter is intentionally narrow. It teaches how to read sets, functions, quantifiers, indices, and shapes without drafting probability or project material. Later chapters remain subject to curriculum and cross-subject review.
