# Subject roadmap

The roadmap follows the twelve phrases in the official description. First make
the security problem and data contract explicit. Then move through structured
vulnerability data, graphs, malware, language, traffic, and behavior. The
final three chapters address attacks on ML itself, LLM-assisted operations,
and bounded response. Topics overlap deliberately, but ownership stays clear:
Robotics owns physical control; MLOps owns lifecycle operation; this subject
owns security threats, controls, and adversarial evidence.
