# Glossary

- **Asset:** something whose confidentiality, integrity, availability, or
  accountability matters.
- **Attack vector:** a route by which a weakness can be reached or exploited.
- **Calibration:** agreement between predicted probabilities and observed
  frequencies on comparable cases.
- **Concept drift:** a change in the relationship between observations and the
  target or in the observation distribution.
- **Data leakage:** information available only after the decision, or otherwise
  outside the intended observation process, entering training or evaluation.
- **Evidence contract:** a testable statement of decision, threat, population,
  label, action, costs, and boundaries.
- **Indicator:** a signal associated with a hypothesis; not proof of intent.
- **Threat model:** an explicit account of assets, adversaries, capabilities,
  access, goals, and assumptions.
