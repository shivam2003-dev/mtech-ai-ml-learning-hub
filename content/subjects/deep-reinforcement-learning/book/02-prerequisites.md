# Prerequisites

**Prerequisite:** vectors, matrix products, gradients, the chain rule, and
basic optimisation. **Prerequisite:** expectation, variance, conditional
probability, sampling, and confidence intervals. **Prerequisite:** supervised
learning, loss functions, validation, and regularisation. **Prerequisite:**
Python, NumPy arrays, PyTorch training loops, unit tests, and dependency
isolation. **Supplementary bridge:** Markov chains, finite-horizon reasoning,
and basic queue or buffer complexity.

If these topics are rusty, use the cross-links to Mathematical Foundations,
Statistical Methods, Machine Learning, and Deep Neural Networks before
interpreting a learning curve.
