# Learning outcomes

By the end of the proposed book, a learner should be able to specify a valid
MDP, derive Bellman targets, implement a tabular baseline, explain the deep
RL stability problem, compare major algorithm families, and evaluate claims
across seeds with explicit uncertainty. A learner should also be able to say
when not to deploy an agent: reward misspecification, unsupported actions,
unsafe exploration, and distribution shift are engineering findings, not
footnotes.
