# Deep Reinforcement Learning

> **Curriculum boundary:** the brochure verifies this exact title and its
> compulsory Semester 2 placement, but it does not provide topic-level units.
> The twelve-chapter sequence is therefore a reviewed educational proposal.
> Every proposed topic is labelled **Prerequisite**, **Supplementary**,
> **Advanced extension**, or **Industry application** in the subject plan.

Deep reinforcement learning (DRL) combines sequential decision-making with
neural function approximation. The book starts with an interaction contract,
then builds Markov decision processes, Bellman reasoning, sampling, deep value
methods, policy gradients, actor-critic learning, continuous control,
model-based and offline methods, and responsible evaluation. The aim is not to
memorise algorithm names: it is to understand which information is available,
which estimate is being updated, and which failure mode a design invites.

## Available reference chapter

1. [Sequential Decisions: From Rewards to Learning Contracts](./chapters/01-sequential-decisions-and-the-drl-experimental-contract/) — a compact narrative introduction with formal returns, a Bellman derivation, a tested tabular Q-learning loop, and a reproducible study lab.

## Planned book sequence

The remaining eleven chapters are planned in the Subject Lead bundle: MDPs and
Bellman reasoning; Monte Carlo and temporal-difference control; function
approximation; DQN and its extensions; policy gradients; actor-critic and PPO;
continuous control; model-based learning; offline RL; safety and evaluation;
and scaling, multi-agent systems, and deployment.
