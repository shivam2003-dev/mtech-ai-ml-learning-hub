# Glossary

- **Action:** a choice made by the agent at a decision time.
- **Agent:** the decision-maker that maps observations or states to actions.
- **Bellman equation:** a recursive relation between current value, immediate reward, and future value.
- **Episode:** one rollout that ends at termination or an external truncation.
- **Environment:** the process that returns rewards and next observations after actions.
- **Markov decision process:** a formal sequential decision model with a conditional-independence assumption.
- **Off-policy:** learning a target policy from data generated by another behaviour policy.
- **Policy:** a distribution or function that selects actions.
- **Return:** discounted cumulative future reward.
- **Temporal-difference error:** the difference between a bootstrapped target and a current estimate.
