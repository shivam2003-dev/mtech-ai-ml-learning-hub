# Architecting AI Systems

> **Curriculum boundary:** The official BITS Pilani WILP brochure lists the
> topics below on printed page 15: end-to-end AI system design and architecture
> patterns, AI system components, architectural styles, agentic AI architecture,
> ADLC, MCP/A2A gateways and agent protocols, system-level design principles,
> scalability patterns, performance optimization, resiliency patterns,
> domain-specific architectural blueprints, infrastructure integration,
> production considerations, and governance. The Semester 2–3 elective mapping
> remains configurable. Chapter ordering and additional tools are labelled
> supplementary or advanced extension rather than presented as official units.

## Reference chapter

1. [From a Model to a Governed AI Product](./chapters/01-from-a-model-to-a-governed-ai-product/) — a tested, source-bounded first chapter connecting the official topics through one architecture case.

## Proposed book sequence

1. From a Model to a Governed AI Product
2. Components, Contracts, and Architectural Styles
3. The AI Development Life Cycle (ADLC)
4. Agentic AI, MCP, A2A, and Tool Trust Boundaries
5. System-Level Design Principles and Domain Blueprints
6. Scalability and Performance Engineering
7. Resiliency, Safety, and Graceful Degradation
8. Infrastructure Integration and Production Readiness
9. Governance, Evaluation, and Responsible Change
10. Capstone: An Architecture Decision Record for an AI System

Only Chapter 1 is authored in this vertical slice. The remaining chapters are
planned and will be released only after independent technical, citation,
mathematics, code, editorial, and rendered accessibility reviews.
