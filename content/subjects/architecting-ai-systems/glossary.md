# Glossary

- **ADLC:** AI Development Life Cycle; an evidence path from framing through
  retirement.
- **Architecture style:** a recurring way to place responsibilities and
  communication boundaries, such as modular, service-oriented, event-driven, or
  agentic composition.
- **Agent gateway:** a policy and protocol boundary through which an agent
  discovers or invokes typed capabilities.
- **Backpressure:** a mechanism that prevents producers from overwhelming a
  downstream component.
- **Governance:** roles, rules, evidence, recourse, and controls that make an AI
  system accountable over time.
- **Resiliency:** preserving an acceptable outcome while components or
  assumptions fail.
- **SLO:** a target for an observed service-level indicator over a stated window.
