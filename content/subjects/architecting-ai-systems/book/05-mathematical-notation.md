# Mathematical notation

- $x$: validated input; $\hat{y}$: model output; $\theta$: model artefact.
- $\lambda$: arrival rate; $\mu$: service rate; $\rho=\lambda/\mu$: utilisation.
- $W$: approximate time in an M/M/1 system; $A$: availability.

These equations are educational approximations. State assumptions and measure
the real workload before making a production capacity claim.
