# Further reading

- Agrawal, Imieliński, and Swami (1993), the foundational association-rule
  formulation.
- Han, Pei, and Yin (2000), FP-Growth and pattern-growth reasoning.
- Aggarwal (2015), *Data Mining: The Textbook*, for broad coverage of complex
  data, streams, graphs, and outliers.
- The official ACM SIGKDD resource pages for conferences, datasets, and
  community context. Verify current links before using them in a submission.
