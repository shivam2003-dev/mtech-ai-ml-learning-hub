# Preface

Advanced Data Mining is about finding structure in data whose shape does not
fit a single rectangular table. This book starts with a reproducible record,
then expands the question to patterns, sequences, graphs, streams, locations,
time, and social relationships. Every extension keeps the same discipline:
define what was observed, what was not observed, and what a discovered pattern
is allowed to claim.

This is an educational interpretation of the official BITS Pilani WILP course
description. University topic phrases are marked as official; added practice,
prerequisites, and industry examples are marked as supplementary or industry
application.
