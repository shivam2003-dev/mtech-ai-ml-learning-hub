# Mathematical notation

We write a record as $x$, a dataset as $D$, an itemset as $X$, support as
$\operatorname{supp}(X)$, confidence as $\operatorname{conf}(X\Rightarrow Y)$,
and lift as $\operatorname{lift}(X\Rightarrow Y)$. Bold lowercase symbols are
vectors, uppercase letters are sets or matrices, and $t$ denotes an observation
time. Equations are rendered with MathJax on the site.
