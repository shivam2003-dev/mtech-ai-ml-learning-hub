# Glossary

- **Association rule:** a directional conditional summary such as $X\Rightarrow Y$.
- **Attribute:** a measured field of a record.
- **Closed itemset:** an itemset with no superset having the same support.
- **Confidence:** empirical conditional frequency of a consequent given an antecedent.
- **Data contract:** explicit unit, availability, pattern, and permitted-use agreement.
- **Downward closure:** every subset of a frequent itemset is frequent.
- **Itemset:** an unordered set of categorical tokens.
- **Lift:** confidence divided by consequent support.
- **Pattern:** a formal repeated structure, such as an itemset, sequence, motif, or cluster.
- **Support:** fraction of transactions containing an itemset.
- **Transaction:** one observation represented as a set or sequence of items.
