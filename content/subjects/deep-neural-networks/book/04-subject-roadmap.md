# Subject roadmap

The proposed sequence maps the twelve exact brochure topic phrases one-to-one:

1. introduction to neural networks;
2. linear neural networks for regression and classification;
3. deep feedforward neural networks;
4. backpropagation;
5. deep network training;
6. regularization and optimization;
7. transfer learning and fine-tuning;
8. convolutional neural networks;
9. recurrent neural networks;
10. attention mechanisms;
11. transformers;
12. vision transformers.

The sequence moves from computation contracts to fitting, depth, gradients,
training, and regularization before branching into transfer, convolution,
recurrence, attention, transformers, and vision transformers. The order is a
coherent learning design, not a university-prescribed unit order.

The core chapter plan totals 184 hours. Readiness, one selected project path,
and cumulative review bring the proposed subject pathway to 236 hours. Project
options are alternatives; their hours must not be added as though every project
were mandatory.
