# Glossary

- **Activation:** a transformation applied to a pre-activation, often
  elementwise.
- **Architecture:** the operations, connectivity, and structural dimensions of
  a model.
- **Automatic differentiation:** systematic derivative evaluation through
  recorded primitive operations.
- **Bias:** an additive affine parameter.
- **Computation graph:** a directed representation of values and operations.
- **Differentiable program:** a program whose relevant output sensitivities can
  be computed through its operations, subject to stated boundaries.
- **Hidden representation:** an intermediate coordinate representation; not
  automatically a human-interpretable concept.
- **Parameter:** a value adjusted by the learning procedure.
- **Pre-activation:** a unit's affine value before its activation function.
- **ReLU:** the elementwise function \(\max(0,x)\).
