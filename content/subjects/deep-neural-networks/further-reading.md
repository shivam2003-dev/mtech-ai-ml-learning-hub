# Further reading

1. Goodfellow, Bengio, and Courville, *Deep Learning*, for the mathematical and
   architectural map.
2. Zhang, Lipton, Li, and Smola, *Dive into Deep Learning*, for an executable
   progression.
3. PyTorch's model-building and autograd tutorials for framework mechanics.
4. Rosenblatt (1958) for carefully bounded historical context.
5. LeCun, Bengio, and Hinton (2015) for a broad review whose individual claims
   should be followed to primary work.
6. Mitchell et al. (2019) for model-reporting structure.

Use the chapter bibliography and subject references for canonical URLs and
identifiers.
