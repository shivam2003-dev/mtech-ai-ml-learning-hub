# Deep Neural Networks

> **Curriculum status:** the official brochure supplies the course title,
> Semester 2 placement, and one course-topic description. It does not define
> units, prerequisites, outcomes, hours, tools, datasets, assessment weights,
> or chapter ordering. The twelve-chapter roadmap is a labelled educational
> interpretation, not an invented official syllabus.

The official description names: introduction to neural networks; linear neural
networks for regression and classification; deep feedforward neural networks;
backpropagation; deep network training; regularization and optimization;
transfer learning and fine-tuning; convolutional neural networks; recurrent
neural networks; attention mechanisms; transformers; and vision transformers.
The book maps each exact phrase to one proposed chapter so coverage stays
auditable.

## Published reference chapter

1. [Neural Networks as Differentiable Programs](./chapters/01-neural-networks-as-differentiable-programs/) — **Official topic: Introduction to neural networks**, with labelled Supplementary computation-graph depth and Industry application reporting guidance.

7. Transfer Learning and Fine-Tuning
8. Convolutional Neural Networks
9. Recurrent Neural Networks and Sequence Memory

unavailable until their citation, mathematics, code, editorial, rendering, and
