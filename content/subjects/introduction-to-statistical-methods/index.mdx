# Introduction to Statistical Methods

> **Curriculum status:** the official programme brochure confirms this subject
> title and its Semester 1 compulsory placement. It does not provide a detailed
> topic list. The roadmap and reference chapter are therefore a **provisional
> educational interpretation**, visibly classified as prerequisite or
> supplementary material rather than official university units.

Statistical work begins before a formula is selected. A trustworthy analysis
must say which population matters, how observations entered the data, what was
measured, which quantity answers the question, and what evidence would change
the decision. This book develops those habits before moving into sampling
distributions, estimation, intervals, tests, regression, Bayesian modelling,
robustness, and production experimentation.

## Available reference chapter

1. [From Questions to Statistical Evidence](./chapters/01-from-questions-to-statistical-evidence/) — **Prerequisite with Supplementary derivation**. Populations, samples, variables, estimands, measurement, selection bias, provenance, exploratory work, confirmatory work, and a tested sampling audit.
