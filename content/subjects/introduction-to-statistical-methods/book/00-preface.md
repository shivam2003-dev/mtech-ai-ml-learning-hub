# Preface

This provisional book teaches statistical reasoning for empirical AI and
machine-learning work. The programme brochure confirms the subject title and
Semester 1 placement but supplies no detailed units. The book therefore labels
its material as Prerequisite, Supplementary, Advanced extension, or Industry
application and never presents the proposed outline as official syllabus text.

The first reference chapter starts with questions, measurement, sampling, and
estimands because a sophisticated calculation cannot repair a badly defined
target or an untrustworthy data-generating process.
