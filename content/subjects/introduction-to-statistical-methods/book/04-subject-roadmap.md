# Subject Roadmap

The approved planning bundle proposes this dependency sequence:

```text
questions, measurement, samples, and estimands
  -> probability-readiness diagnostic
  -> sampling designs and sampling distributions
  -> estimation and likelihood
  -> intervals, resampling, tests, and power
  -> multiple inference and nonparametric methods
  -> linear models and ANOVA
  -> Bayesian, robustness, missing-data, and industry extensions
```

Only **From Questions to Statistical Evidence** is drafted in this vertical
slice. The remaining chapters are planned, not published.
