# Learning Outcomes

The proposed reference-slice outcomes are:

- turn a vague product question into an auditable statistical question;
- distinguish a population, sampling frame, sample, and observational unit;
- classify variables with their meaning, units, and measurement process;
- define an estimand before selecting an estimator;
- explain when a sample mean is or is not representative;
- identify selection, measurement, leakage, missingness, and multiplicity risks;
- separate exploratory findings from confirmatory claims;
- implement and test a small sampling audit without overstating its conclusions.
