# Further Reading

For an accessible continuation, use *OpenIntro Statistics*. For postgraduate
inference, use MIT 18.650 and Larry Wasserman's *All of Statistics*. For
engineering diagnostics, use the NIST/SEMATECH handbook.

The current chapter's transparency reading is Nosek et al. (2015). Treat it as
a standards proposal rather than proof that following a checklist makes an
analysis correct.
