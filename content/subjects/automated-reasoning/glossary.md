# Glossary

- **Clause:** A disjunction of literals.
- **CNF:** A conjunction of clauses.
- **Countermodel:** A model showing that an entailment claim fails.
- **DPLL:** A backtracking SAT procedure with propagation.
- **Entailment:** Every model of assumptions also satisfies a conclusion.
- **Literal:** A proposition or its negation.
- **Model:** An assignment that satisfies a formula or set of formulas.
- **Resolution:** A sound inference rule eliminating complementary literals.
- **SAT:** The question of whether a formula has a model.
- **SMT:** Satisfiability modulo a background theory.
- **Tautology:** A formula true under every assignment.
- **Unsatisfiable core:** A subset of constraints with no model.
