# Automated Reasoning

> **Curriculum boundary:** The official BITS Pilani WILP brochure verifies
> *Automated Reasoning* as an elective title in the configurable Semester 2–3
> pool, but provides no unit list, outcomes, prerequisites, or tools for this
> title. The roadmap below is therefore a proposed educational interpretation.
> Every topic is labelled **Prerequisite**, **Supplementary**, **Advanced
> extension**, or **Industry application**; none is presented as an official
> university unit.

## Reference chapter

1. [A Solver Must Explain Its Answer](./chapters/01-a-solver-must-explain-its-answer/) — **Supplementary interpretation** of automated reasoning through propositional proof search, resolution, and model checking.

## Proposed book sequence

1. A Solver Must Explain Its Answer
2. Terms, Formulas, and Unification
3. Resolution and Refutation in First-Order Logic
4. SAT, DPLL, and Conflict-Driven Search
5. SMT and Theory-Aware Reasoning
6. Model Checking, Planning, and Verification
7. Proof Certificates and Trustworthy Solvers
8. Reproducible Automated-Reasoning Research

Only Chapter 1 is authored in this vertical slice. Future chapters remain
planned until their source, mathematics, code, editorial, rendering, and
accessibility reviews pass.
