1. [A Robot Learns in a Loop](./chapters/01-a-robot-learns-in-a-loop/) — **Supplementary interpretation** of the title, with explicit boundaries around perception, estimation, planning, control, and learning.

1. A Robot Learns in a Loop
2. State, Sensors, and Coordinate Frames
3. Learning Perception for Action
4. Dynamics, Planning, and Model-Based Learning
5. Reinforcement and Imitation Learning on Robots
6. Sim-to-Real Transfer and Safety Cases
7. Multi-Robot and Human-Robot Learning
8. Reproducible Robot-Learning Research
