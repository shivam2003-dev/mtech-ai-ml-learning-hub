# Glossary

- **Action:** a command sent to a robot or simulator.
- **Closed loop:** a system that uses observations or estimates to update its next action.
- **Observation:** a sensor measurement available to the decision process.
- **Policy:** a rule or learned distribution for selecting actions.
- **Sim-to-real:** transfer of behaviour from simulation to physical hardware.
- **State:** variables sufficient, under a chosen model, to predict relevant future behaviour.
