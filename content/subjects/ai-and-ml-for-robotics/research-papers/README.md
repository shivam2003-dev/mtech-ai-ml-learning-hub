# Research reading path

This provisional reading path is grouped by idea, not by an official syllabus:

- Kober, Bagnell, and Peters (2013), *Reinforcement Learning in Robotics: A Survey*, DOI `10.1109/MRA.2012.2226854`.
- Levine et al. (2016), *End-to-End Training of Deep Visuomotor Policies*, JMLR 17.
- Tobin et al. (2017), *Domain Randomization for Transferring Deep Neural Networks from Simulation to the Real World*, IROS, DOI `10.1109/IROS.2017.8202133`.
- OpenAI et al. (2019), *Solving Rubik's Cube with a Robot Hand*, arXiv:1910.07113.
- Kalashnikov et al. (2018), *QT-Opt: Scalable Deep Reinforcement Learning for Vision-Based Robotic Manipulation*, arXiv:1806.10293.
- Sutton and Barto (2018), *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*, 2nd ed., MIT Press.
- Siciliano et al. (2010), *Robotics: Modelling, Planning and Control*, Springer.
- Thrun, Burgard, and Fox (2005), *Probabilistic Robotics*, MIT Press.

Identifiers and venue metadata are pending independent citation review. No item
should be interpreted as an official course requirement.
