# Mathematical notation

Vectors are bold lower-case symbols such as $\mathbf{x}$; matrices are bold
upper-case symbols such as $\mathbf{A}$. A state $\mathbf{s}_t$ describes the
hidden situation at time $t$, an observation $\mathbf{o}_t$ is a sensor reading,
and an action $\mathbf{a}_t$ is a command. A policy $\pi(\mathbf{a}\mid
\mathbf{o})$ maps information to an action distribution. Expectations use
$\mathbb{E}$, and a superscript denotes a coordinate frame when needed.
