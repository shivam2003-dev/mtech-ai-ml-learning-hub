# Preface

Robotics makes the boundary between an algorithm and the world impossible to
ignore. A model can be statistically accurate yet unsafe when a camera is
occluded, a floor is slippery, or a person moves differently from the training
data. This book keeps perception, estimation, planning, control, learning, and
operational responsibility in one conversation.

The brochure gives us the official subject title and elective placement, not a
detailed syllabus. We therefore preserve that uncertainty in every chapter.
Proposed teaching material is marked as prerequisite, supplementary, advanced
extension, or industry application. It must never be mistaken for an official
unit until an authoritative course document is available.

The learning sequence follows intuition, a small physical story, formal model,
derivation, executable experiment, evaluation, failure analysis, and research
connection. The examples are deliberately modest so that a reader can inspect
every assumption before moving to a simulator or robot.
