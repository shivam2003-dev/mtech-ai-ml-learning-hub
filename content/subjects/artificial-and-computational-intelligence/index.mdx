# Artificial and Computational Intelligence

> **Curriculum status:** the official programme brochure confirms this subject
> title and its compulsory Semester 1 placement. It does not provide a detailed
> unit or topic list. The roadmap below is therefore a **provisional educational
> interpretation** whose topics are explicitly classified as prerequisite,
> supplementary, advanced extension, or industry application.

Artificial and computational intelligence is not a contest to find a single
definition of “intelligent.” It is a disciplined way to decide what a system
must observe, which actions it may take, how success and harm will be measured,
which computational method is appropriate, and what evidence supports the
resulting claim. The planned book connects rational agency, search, knowledge
representation, reasoning under uncertainty, learning, evolutionary methods,
collective intelligence, responsible evaluation, and production practice.

## Available reference chapter

1. [Intelligence as Computation: Problems, Agents, and Evidence](./chapters/01-intelligence-as-computation/) — **Prerequisite**, with supplementary formalism and an industry-application study lab.

## Planned roadmap

The verified subject plan proposes twelve chapters. The first establishes a
common contract for problem formulation and evidence. Later chapters will cover
state-space search, adversarial search, constraint satisfaction, knowledge and
reasoning, uncertainty, fuzzy systems, evolutionary computation, swarm
intelligence, hybrid methods, responsible evaluation, and deployment. Those
chapters remain planned rather than silently presented as official brochure
units.
