# Further reading

Start with Turing's historical operational question, then compare a modern AI
textbook's agent framing with Bezdek's discussion of computational-intelligence
boundaries. Read evaluation and risk resources after writing your own task and
claim cards so that abstract cautions can be connected to a concrete system.

The structured path in `research-papers/reading-path.yaml` records identifiers,
difficulty, access, relevance, and verification status.
