# Glossary

**Agent:** a selected decision boundary that receives observations and produces
actions.

**Computational intelligence:** a historically contested family label often
used for adaptive methods including neural, evolutionary, and fuzzy systems.

**Environment:** relevant external state and dynamics.

**Feasible action:** an action satisfying all declared hard constraints.

**Observation:** information available to an agent, potentially incomplete,
delayed, or noisy.

**Rational action:** an action selected appropriately relative to stated
information and objectives.
