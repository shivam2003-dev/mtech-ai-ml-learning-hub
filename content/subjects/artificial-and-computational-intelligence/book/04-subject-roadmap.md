# Subject roadmap

The validated plan proposes twelve educational chapters: task formulation;
state-space and heuristic search; adversarial search; constraint satisfaction;
knowledge representation; uncertainty; fuzzy systems; evolutionary
computation; swarm intelligence; hybrid methods; responsible evaluation; and
deployment. These are not represented as official brochure units.

Current publication state: Chapter 1 is a reference implementation in review;
Chapters 2–12 remain planned.
