# Mathematical notation

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $s_t$ | environment state at time $t$ |
| $o_t$ | observation available at time $t$ |
| $\mathcal{A}(o)$ | legal actions after observation $o$ |
| $F(o)$ | feasible actions after hard constraints |
| $J(a\mid o)$ | declared score or cost of action $a$ |
| $a^\star$ | selected minimum-cost action |
| $\arg\min$ | set of arguments attaining the minimum |

Scalar units and assumptions must be stated at first use. Vectors, matrices,
probability distributions, and alternative notation will be introduced in the
chapters that require them.
